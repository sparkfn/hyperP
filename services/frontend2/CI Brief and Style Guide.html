01 Brand foundations

Positioning

HyperP is an entity resolution workspace for teams whose data lives in many systems. Where most CRMs hide duplicates and conflicts, HyperP treats them as first-class objects — clusters, suspects, lineage — and lets operators reason about identity directly.

Audience

Data operators, fraud and KYC analysts, and engineers building on top of the resolution graph. The brand should feel capable, fast, and slightly opinionated — closer to a developer tool than a sales CRM.

Mission

Give every record one true identity, with every claim traceable to a source.

Principles

P · 01Density over decorationOperators scan thousands of rows a day. Earn every pixel. No illustration, no gradients, no chrome that does not carry information.
P · 02Show the seam, not the smoothingConflicts, duplicates, and lineage are visible by default. We do not auto-merge what we cannot prove.
P · 03Every value has a sourceIf a field is on screen, the system it came from is one glance away. Source chips are non-negotiable.
P · 04Read like an engineer wrote itIDs are monospace. Numbers are tabular. Labels are sparing. Tone is precise, never cute.
P · 05Two modes, one systemLight and dark are equal citizens. Every token must work in both; we never hard-code colors.

02 Voice & tone

HyperP speaks the way a careful operator would brief a colleague: specific, qualified, and never breathless. Sentences carry numbers when numbers are available. Marketing words are demerits.

Do
  • Lead with the noun and the count: "4 likely duplicates of Hanafie."
  • Qualify confidence: likely, suspect, matched on.
  • Name the source: "ingested from eko_phppos".
  • Use lowercase for system identifiers and ALL-CAPS only for section labels.
"Cluster C-001 · 4 likely duplicates · matched on phone +6589193069."
Don't
  • Don't invent excitement: no exclamation marks, no "great news".
  • Don't use emoji or rounded illustrations in product surfaces.
  • Don't hide ambiguity behind a checkmark; show the conflict.
  • Don't call records "users", "leads", or "contacts" — they are persons.
"🎉 You have new matches! Click here to magically merge them."

03 Logo & wordmark

The mark is a square tile with an uppercase H, paired with the wordmark HyperP. It is built from the same primitives as the product UI — a 6 px-radius surface, the strong text color, and one letterform. No bespoke letter drawing; no ornament.

Construction

  • Tile: 30 × 30 at 14 px text, scales linearly. Corner radius 7 px at 30 px, 0.23 × tile size at larger.
  • Background: --text-strong in light mode, --accent (indigo) in dark mode.
  • Foreground: pure white. Letter weight 700, tracking -0.02em.
  • Wordmark gap: 10 px. Wordmark uses the body font at 15 px / 600.

Clearspace

Reserve space equal to the tile's height on all sides. Do not letterspace the wordmark; do not outline the tile; do not place on textured backgrounds.

Type rules

  • Headings use tight tracking (-0.2 to -0.4 px); body text uses default tracking.
  • Labels (column heads, section eyebrows) are always UPPERCASE · 600 · letter-spacing 0.05–0.08em · text-muted.
  • Numbers use font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums wherever they sit in a column.
  • Monospace is mandatory for IDs, NRIC, phone, hex, postal codes, and currency totals in tables.
  • Line length caps at 68ch for paragraphs; longer becomes hard to scan at 13 px.

Popovers

One popover surface for the entire product: --bg-surface with --border-soft, radius 6 px, shadow 0 12px 32px rgba(15,23,42,0.18), padding 6 px. Header is a UPPERCASE label at 10 px, optional count on the right. List items are 12 px text with 5–6 px vertical padding, radius 4 px, hover --bg-surface-2.

Tables

  • Header row: --bg-surface-2 background, 10 px UPPERCASE labels, 0.5 letter-spacing.
  • Body rows: --bg-surface with 1 px solid --border-soft bottom rule. No zebra striping.
  • Hover: --row-hover background; active row gets a 2 px left border in --accent and --row-active background.
  • Right-align numeric columns; mono-format IDs and totals.
  • Padding: 10 px 14 px per cell.

09 Domain patterns

The cluster banner

Cluster C-001 · 4 likely duplicates of "Hanafie Bin Dolklifie" · matched on phone +6589193069

Appears in the table immediately after the first row of the cluster. Always uses warn tokens. Reserved exclusively for resolution conflicts — never for general informational messages.

Source-of-truth notation

Wherever a value originates from one of several sources, show it as value Fundbox. In comparison tables, mark the chosen value with a CHOSEN badge in --good tones. The policy that produced the choice (e.g. "Fundbox-first") is stated once in the section header, not on every row.

Multi-value fields

When a field has more than one value (multiple phones, emails, addresses), show the primary and a +N chip. The chip opens a popover listing all values, with the primary highlighted in a --good-soft band and a PRIMARY badge.

Empty states

  • Missing single value: em-dash — in --text-faint. Never "N/A" or "null".
  • Empty list: a single line of 11 px muted text — "No connections" — no illustrations.
  • Empty table: header row only + a 14 px muted message in the body area.

Confidence visualisation

Connection strength is a thin 4 px bar in --accent, sitting against a --bg-surface-3 track. Completeness uses a wider bar (6 px) and the colour shifts by band, matching the avatar ring. Never use red/green/yellow icons for the same data — pick one channel and stick to it.

10 Layout & density

Listing — Direction B

  • Three-column shell: nav 224 px · main fluid · focus drawer 400 px (overlay).
  • Top bar: logo + global search + entity switcher + user.
  • Toolbar: search input · pinned filter pills · More · View.
  • Table: 9 columns; bulk action bar appears on selection; cluster banners interleave with rows.
  • Pagination at the foot of the table; rows-per-page select uses --border-soft.

Detail — Variation A

  • Two-column: identity 360 px · tabbed body fluid.
  • Header carries the breadcrumb, mode toggle and primary actions.
  • Right column opens with a 4-up KPI strip; tabs default to Orders.
  • Cluster review surfaces in the identity column only when the person is in a cluster.

Density rules

  • Row height in tables: 44 px (10 px vertical padding + 12 px line-height + content).
  • Card padding defaults to 14 px; compact variant 10 px; comfortable 18 px (reserved for the identity hero only).
  • Section gap within a column: 12 px; between columns: 16 px.
  • No purely decorative whitespace: every gap maps to a token on the 4 px grid.

11 Data visualisation

Charts use a single accent (--accent) plus the source palette when category is the dimension. Axes are minimal: no gridlines unless the chart is > 200 px tall; tick labels at 9–10 px --text-faint; tabular-nums.

12 Applications

The system is product-first. Marketing and document surfaces inherit from it rather than the other way around.

SurfaceModeNotes
Product app (Persons workspace)Dark default, light optionalOperators run long sessions. Dark reduces fatigue; light is shipped for accessibility and document export.

Don'ts (gallery)

  • Don't paint backgrounds with gradients.
  • Don't use rounded illustrations of people, robots, or hands.
  • Don't introduce a second accent color without retiring the first.
  • Don't render IDs in a proportional font.
  • Don't replace the source chip with the source's own logo.
  • Don't show a green check next to an unverified field.
